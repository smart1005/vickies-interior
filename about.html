✦ Who We Are

The Story Behind
Vickie's Interior

Vickie's Interior was born from a passion for creating spaces that don't just look beautiful — but feel like home. Founded by Vickie herself, the business grew from a simple love of transforming ordinary rooms into extraordinary living experiences.

Based in Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria, we have spent over 8 years curating premium furniture, art pieces, and accessories that bring elegance and personality to every space we touch.

From office chairs to gold figurines, wall arts to luxury carpets — every piece in our collection is carefully selected to meet the highest standards of quality and design.
